# The Missing Evidence

## Mission

A suspicious digital artifact was recovered from a compromised system.

The investigator who collected it believes that important information may be hidden inside the file. Your task is to examine the evidence carefully and determine what happened.

## Objective

Analyze the provided evidence and recover the hidden flag.

You should:

1. Identify the actual type of the file.
2. Inspect its metadata and structure.
3. Search for hidden or unusual content.
4. Recover the final flag.

## Difficulty

Medium
